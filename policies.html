Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how we handle it.

Information we collect

When you contact us or place an order, we may collect:

  • Your name and contact details, such as your WhatsApp number
  • Your project requirements, instructions, and any files you send us
  • Payment confirmation details, such as a bank transfer receipt

Why we collect it

We use this information only to understand your request, prepare a quote, deliver your work, confirm payment, and support you afterwards.

Who we share it with

We never sell your information or share your personal details and project files with third parties.

The only exception is where we are required to do so by law. Our conversations take place on WhatsApp, which is a separate service governed by its own privacy policy.

Local storage

This website stores a single item in your browser's local storage: your language preference (English or Arabic). It is used only to remember your choice and is never used for tracking.

Privacy questions

If you have a question about your data, or would like us to delete what we hold about you, message us on WhatsApp and we'll respond promptly.

Terms of Service

By ordering from AcademiPro, you agree to the terms below.

Our service

AcademiPro provides the academic support, tutoring, and tech services listed on this website. The scope, price, and deadline of each project are agreed with you in advance on WhatsApp.

How ordering works

  1. 1

    Message

    Send us your requirements and deadline on WhatsApp.

  2. 2

    Quote

    We reply with a price and timeline.

  3. 3

    Confirm

    You approve the quote and we share payment details.

  4. 4

    Delivery

    We deliver your completed work by the agreed deadline.

Payment

  • We accept bank transfer (IBAN). Payment details are sent after you confirm the quote.
  • For larger projects, a partial upfront payment may be required before work begins. The balance is paid as agreed in your quote.

Revisions

We offer free revisions until you are satisfied with the delivered work. Revisions cover changes within the requirements originally agreed; new or additional requirements may be quoted separately.

Use of delivered work

Please note

All work is delivered for your own reference and study use. You are responsible for how you use what we deliver, and for complying with the academic policies of your own school, college, or university. AcademiPro is not responsible for any consequences that result from how delivered work is used.

Refund Policy

We want you to feel confident when you order. Here is how refunds work at each stage of a project.

Before work starts

Full refund

If you cancel before we begin working on your project, you receive a full refund of any amount paid.

After work starts, before delivery

Partial refund

If you cancel once work is underway, we refund the portion that does not cover work already completed.

After final delivery

No refund

Once the final work has been delivered, payments are non-refundable, except where the delivered work does not match what was agreed.

If delivered work doesn't match what was agreed

Tell us and we will first correct it through free revisions. If we are unable to put it right, you are eligible for a refund.

How to request a refund

  • Message us on WhatsApp with your order details and the reason for your request.
  • We'll review it and confirm the refund amount with you.
  • Approved refunds are returned by bank transfer to the account you paid from.
